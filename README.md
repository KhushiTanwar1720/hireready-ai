# HireReady AI

> Practice Smarter. Get Hired Faster.

HireReady AI is an AI-powered interview preparation platform designed to help candidates prepare for Technical and HR interviews through personalized questions, AI-powered mock interviews, answer analysis, actionable feedback, and progress tracking.

## 🚀 Overview

Interview preparation can be difficult when candidates rely on generic resources, have limited access to mock interviewers, and do not receive instant feedback.

HireReady AI addresses these challenges by providing an interactive platform where candidates can:

- Prepare according to their target role
- Upload their resume for personalized preparation
- Practice Technical and HR questions
- Participate in AI-powered mock interviews
- Receive detailed feedback on their answers
- Identify weak areas
- Track interview performance over time

## ✨ Key Features

### 🤖 AI-Powered Mock Interviews
Practice realistic Technical and HR interviews with an AI interviewer.

### 🎯 Personalized Questions
Generate questions based on the candidate's role, experience, resume, topic, and difficulty level.

### 📄 Resume-Based Preparation
Upload a resume and receive role-specific preparation insights and recommended topics.

### 💬 Answer Feedback
Get actionable feedback on:

- Technical Knowledge
- Answer Quality
- Communication
- Confidence
- Problem Solving

### 📚 Question Bank
Practice questions across:

**Technical**
- Data Structures & Algorithms
- DBMS
- Operating Systems
- Computer Networks

**HR**
- Behavioral
- Situational
- Personality

### 📊 Progress Tracking
Track:

- Interview scores
- Technical performance
- HR performance
- Communication
- Confidence
- Problem solving
- Practice activity

### 📝 Interview History
Review previous interviews, scores, duration, difficulty, and feedback.
